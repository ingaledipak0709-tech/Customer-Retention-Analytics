# Customer-Retention-Analytics

A data analytics project focused on understanding customer behavior, retention patterns, and customer attrition using Python-based Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

## Overview

Customer Lifecycle Insights analyzes customer data to identify patterns related to customer retention and attrition. The project follows a structured data analytics workflow, from data preparation and exploration to visualization and business insights.

The analysis helps understand customer characteristics and factors that may be associated with customer churn, providing a foundation for improving customer retention strategies.

## Objectives

* Understand customer behavior and characteristics
* Analyze customer retention and attrition patterns
* Explore relationships between customer attributes
* Identify important trends and patterns
* Create meaningful data visualizations
* Generate actionable business insights
